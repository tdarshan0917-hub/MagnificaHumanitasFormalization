## Positive evidence and scope checks

The help page provides disability/accommodation assistance (E001-S2, Job Seekers with Disabilities). The assessment page describes recruiter review (E001-S4, Why do I need to complete an assessment). Neither establishes a post-decision remedy by itself.

The applicant privacy policy's California section describes conditional data-correction and automated-processing objection rights with a request channel (E001-S5, California notice and exercise instructions). This is positive disclosure for that stated population; it is not counted as NYC access or independently validated legal advice.

The audit page says selection is not solely determined by one score tier (E001-S1). That is relevant counterevidence against a simplified story of automatic top-tier hiring. No statistical discrimination finding or individual outcome is inferred from the audit numbers.

## What this pilot contributes

The codebook needs separate fields for support, review, outcome challenge, alternative selection, accommodation and geographically scoped privacy rights. This is a concrete improvement over a single 'appeal exists' checkbox.

The review kit can ask for an internal case-owner record, challenge procedure, reviewer remit, transfer receipt and reassessment confirmation to resolve the unknowns. These are proposed evidence requests; they were not sent to Citizens. A hypothetical missing-handoff repair will not be attributed to Citizens as an observed defect.
