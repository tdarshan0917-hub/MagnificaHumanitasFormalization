# Local Law 144 employer evidence update

9 November 2026 · Build 0.3

The documentary sample now has 20 employer groups. It contains five deep reviews from build 0.2 and 15 new quick scans. The new records cover employer identity, candidate contact, review role, case assignment, reconsideration, alternative selection and accommodation. The evidence register links each observation to a source and records the tool, stage, candidate population and relevant date.

| Example | What the published record supports |
| --- | --- |
| Aon | The NYC notice offers an opt-out. The global privacy notice describes conditional human reconsideration of a solely automated decision. The reviewed pages leave the applicability of that right to the NYC notice tool unresolved. |
| Burlington Stores | The early-career notice gives named NYC applicants instructions to request alternative selection or accommodation. It describes a response to that request. The scope of the procedure is the request itself. |
| DoorDash | The job posting identifies the period when DoorDash used Covey Scout for Inbound and gives 30 April 2026 as the end date. The record preserves that historical scope. |
| Morgan Stanley | The tracker points to a URL that now serves a July 2026 audit. The ledger records the version read. The applicant policy describes personnel reviewing scores before employment decisions. |

The bounded searches finished five groups short of the planned 25. The supplemental discovery queue used a pinned ACLU tracker and retained primary-document eligibility. Vendor product audits, duplicate groups and archive wrappers were logged separately. The accommodation and job-board searches produced no further eligible groups in the returned sets. Some tracker leads remain unverified. A public notice sample favors organizations with discoverable documents and supports description of that sample only.

The 15 new records received a same-assistant source reconciliation. Several sources were available as indexed extracts, and two linked applicant privacy documents exposed landing pages without readable policy text. The register records those limits. Final counts remain withheld until the corpus and source versions are frozen and the primary-tool, any-tool, assessment-result and rejection categories are separated.

The remaining formal questions are F1–F5 in [the target map](FORMAL_TARGETS.md). The employer evidence distinguishes the factual claims a workflow can support from the hypothetical premises a proof can use. The next documentary decision is whether to close at the observed 20 groups or define a new discovery bound for the remaining five.
