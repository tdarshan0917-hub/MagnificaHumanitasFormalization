## Scope and limitations

Only small responsibility/appeal/remedy fragments and bounded examples in the three agreed case domains are implemented. The 28 selected claims are not all encoded. The examples do not establish publishable novelty. A model meeting these small checklists is not thereby compliant with the entire encyclical, any statute, or an actual institution's rules.

The source mapping remains an explicit interpretation. The cases separate appeal from remedy capacity, general staffing from identifiable assignment, assignment from correction authority, and eventual control from timely prevention. The current appeal build also isolates the extra duty needed before exclusion: appeal for affected high-impact persons. Irreversible loss and perfect obedience to a delivered stop remain explicit assumptions of the third model.

The oversight comparison preserves the same named responsibility, person-accessible appeal and remedy-capacity facts. A body with a real review role can still lack remit over the denial. A covered reviewer can lack intervention capacity. Actual exercise, independence safeguards and successful correction are outside this capacity model.

The participation comparison records and amends a community recommendation and demonstrates its influence when no institutional override is used. An explicit override can still select either outcome. Binding the final rule to the recommendation supplies operational control, but no legitimacy or full-subsidiarity conclusion follows.

The developer comparison does not confuse a design-stage assignee with an assignee for every later decision. A downstream gap violates an existing duty. Distinct stage assignees satisfy the fragments, and an additional developer assignee is also compatible. Legal liability, full ethical-design duties and actual production/delegation semantics are not established.

Technical control in the authority case is computed from the review rule. Independent institutional recognition changes without changing that capability. Recognition is not moral or legal legitimacy. The scope/significance audit identifies useful checked dependencies while declining any major-novelty or comprehensive-coverage claim.
