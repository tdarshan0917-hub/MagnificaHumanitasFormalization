## Avoiding circular results

If `Compliant` includes `Provided appeal`, then extracting appeal is a projection. It may support a user interface but is not a substantive theorem. An informative dependency result instead compares theories, identifies a necessary bridge, or supplies paired models differing in one provision while preserving their other stipulated facts.

Every formal theorem must report its explicit theory parameters and named premises. Lean's axiom report alone does not reveal all substantive hypotheses hidden inside structure fields. Both logical-axiom and premise-dependency audits are required.

Explicit finite witnesses now exist for the limited prototype policies and the responsibility-only public-benefits interpretations. They establish satisfaction and separations for those named fragments. No full-theory consistency proof, real-world compliance guarantee, or unique interpretation of the source is claimed.

The high-stakes case adds explicit transition-system witnesses and conditional invariants. Its irreversibility is represented in the transition rules and then proved invariant; it is not derived from the encyclical. Record correction remains possible after loss. No conclusion requires pretending that a possible remedy has already been delivered.

In prototype 0.5 the narrow/broad comparison changes only the appeal trigger. Both policies keep the same identifiable-responsibility trigger and neither adds a remedy-capacity obligation. Actual harm is false in the new employment-style witnesses; high impact and affectedness remain true. Appeal is an accessible review channel, and internal review availability does not establish that the affected person may submit a challenge. These are hypothetical case facts, not findings about a real employer.

Prototype 0.6 adds factual oversight provision, not a new unconditional duty-to-fact rule. The regime fragment requires an actual recognized independent body capable of review, with a nonempty remit in the concrete witness. The decision-level extension additionally requires remit and intervention capacity for the same body and decision. The gap cases satisfy the broad appeal and harm-remedy fragments, hence also the narrow appeal fragment, while failing the stronger oversight condition. They are not full-source compliance witnesses. The repaired case satisfies the joint, explicitly limited fragments. Independence, successful intervention, promptness, expertise and resourcing are not established by a role label; only the stipulated independence and action-capacity facts are modeled here.

Prototype 0.7 treats one community as an actual contributor to review of the existing public-benefit denial. False/true recommendations and outcomes mean deny/grant, not unjust/just. Local revision changes the community's own recorded recommendation. The participation fragment requires accepted and faithfully recorded input, amendment capacity, distinct records for distinct inputs, and an outcome-influence witness when no override is present. The same fragment holds with central control and with binding local recommendations. The latter is an optional comparison, not a preferred policy. Operational final control is the ability to impose either outcome against every institutional override; it is not legitimate governing authority. The fixed Governance record describes the original denial under review, not the later review outcome. Person-facing safeguards and the previously satisfied oversight condition remain fixed.

Prototype 0.8 reuses Governance and the existing policies over a concrete finite stage carrier, Sum Unit PublicBenefits.Decision. The new Unit context is a design review inside the existing public-benefits case. It is not a fourth case domain or a model of every lifecycle stage. The affected/harmed person is attached to the denial; the design context is separately classified as consequential. A rule requires identifiable responsibility at both consequential contexts. Compliance at design alone does not cover an unassigned denial. Named developer-at-design and deployer-at-denial assignments satisfy both existing broad-appeal and harm-remedy fragments; adding a downstream developer assignee also satisfies them. These policies specify existence of a responsible assignee, not a unique actor, legal liability or all duties of the developer. Explained/humanReviewed case fields do not by themselves implement ethical design review, fairness assessment or responsibility toward communities. Those parts of MH-019/038/040 need additional justified semantics; the gap is recorded rather than replaced with a convenient Boolean duty.

Prototype 0.9 uses the existing OversightSystem fields for technical intervention, institutional recognition and remit. The operator's ability to produce review outcomes is proved from the existing decision procedure. Recognized-plus-covered is displayed directly as the mandate proxy; it is not renamed legitimate authority. A capable unrecognized operator and a capable recognized operator both satisfy the same person-facing fragments and coexist with an actionable independent board. This is a specification-independence check, not an empirical or moral discovery. Full legitimacy needs a justified account of the source and basis of authority and remains an explicit interpretation gap.

Prototype 0.10 adds a factual direct-producer field to the existing Governance structure; legacy witnesses default to non-AI direct production, which does not deny their possible use of AI assistance. The producer-only update is displayed explicitly in the delegation theorem statements. Its fixed-policy preservation is a structural consequence of preserving all facts used by Provided. The three existing producer-independent policies also retain their requirements when evaluated after the handoff. The concrete AI-produced case and the assignment-removal case supply the actual contrast. No premise excluding AI from moral responsibility, and no theorem obtained from disjoint human/machine types, is used.

Prototype 0.11 supplies an actual public artifact in the existing benefits case. Minimal disclosure means retrievable, arithmetically consistent decision information and genuinely identified contacts; it does not already require the actual case assignee's identity. The notice computes denial from 47 < 50 and lists the exact safeguards being promised. In the initial case, the published contact is a different person from the assigned case officer. The notice satisfies this declared disclosure fragment and the institution has internal allocation, yet human answerability and the existing responsibility requirement fail. The repaired notice includes the actual assignee, whose identity is now public. A stronger transparency standard that already demands that assignee would reject the initial case; the countermodel does not refute that stronger standard. The ethical-declaration test concerns these enumerated commitments, not every possible ethics statement or all regime-level requirements in §106.


Prototype 0.12 tests explicit case-level fairness in the existing benefits domain. The Bool carrier is finite: one online and one assisted-intake application; all entitlement-relevant information is stipulated equal. The 60/15/50 figures and channel irrelevance are hypothetical. Public arithmetic explanation includes the penalty itself and can therefore reveal, rather than conceal, the violation. Full justice, statistical group fairness, protected-attribute discrimination, data accuracy, comprehension and real-world institutional behavior are not established. The two closed record constructors are concrete witnesses, not a new general fairness ontology.
