## Scope and limitations

Only the small responsibility/appeal/remedy fragment is implemented. The 28 selected claims are not all encoded. The example is intuitive and does not establish publishable novelty. A model meeting this small checklist is not thereby compliant with the entire encyclical, any statute, or an actual institution's rules.

The source mapping remains an explicit interpretation. The strongest supported conclusion is that identified responsibility and an accessible review procedure do not by themselves provide correction capacity in the modeled class.

## Prototype gate

The first prototype supports proceeding narrowly: the source mapping can be inspected, and the appeal/remedy distinction is operationally clarified. These are two method-level criteria, not proof of public impact. The next stage must add a less obvious dependence or a meaningful difference between admissible interpretations; otherwise stop at a clearly documented teaching prototype.
