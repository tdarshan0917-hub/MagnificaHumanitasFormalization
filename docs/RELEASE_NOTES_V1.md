## Research outcome

- Appeal expansion and harm-triggered correction capacity are independent extensions of the shared safeguards policy; an exact characterization states what satisfies all three policies together.
- Case answerability concerns the actual assignee. Published commitments, numerical explanations, a service contact and internal staffing can coexist with a missing identified case assignee.
- Oversight requires same-decision remit and relevant intervention capacity. An exercised appeal-to-review-to-handoff-to-update path is stronger evidence than available powers alone.
- Participation, influence, binding decisions and a local pause are different powers. Two effective external controllers need not supply a local power.
- The objection process is connected to the operative scoring selector under explicit role, authority and candidate-validation conditions. The corrected rule survives every finite modeled revision sequence with the stated enforcement assumptions.
- The ordered admitted-appeal/review/handoff/application path changes an already-stored denial to the computed reassessed grant under exact conditions.
- Identical final administrative flags can conceal different physical outcomes. Delivered stopping protects arbitrary finite continuations only under the stated timing and dynamics assumptions.

The ten fixed target questions and six countermodel questions have examined dispositions. They yield a mix of conditional positives, exact dependencies, invariants, non-implications and repaired models. The original 10–15 independent-positive-result aspiration is not certified; helper theorems and overlapping repairs are not counted as discoveries. The contribution is the inspectable source/interpretation/proof chain and concrete working comparisons.
