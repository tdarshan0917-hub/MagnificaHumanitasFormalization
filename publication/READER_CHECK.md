# Reader comprehension check

Use the one-page summary first, then the public report. Ask someone without formal-methods training to explain these in their own words, without coaching:

1. What part of the source document did this study examine?
2. What does Lean check in this project?
3. Why can a successful review leave the original denial unchanged?
4. What should an institution check before saying the correction is complete?

Expected meaning: selected governance commitments in §§97-111; logical consequences of explicit hypothetical rules; a missing delivery route, authority or applied update can preserve the denial; inspect the changed record and separately track any payment or other delivery.

Ask which words or diagram steps were unclear. Use those answers to improve wording. No independent reader has been recruited or tested as part of this release preparation. The editorial language and consistency checks are not evidence of a human-reader study. This check is an editorial aid, not approval of the research.
