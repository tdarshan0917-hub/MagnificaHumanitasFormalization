## Scope and limitations

Only small responsibility/appeal/remedy fragments and the bounded public-benefits responsibility case are implemented. The 28 selected claims are not all encoded. The examples do not establish publishable novelty. A model meeting these small checklists is not thereby compliant with the entire encyclical, any statute, or an actual institution's rules.

The source mapping remains an explicit interpretation. In addition to the appeal/remedy separation, the new case shows why a general staff contact need not be the case assignee. Identifying the assignee and giving that person correction power are separate conditions.

## Prototype gate

The prototype supports proceeding narrowly. The public-benefits example exposes a mismatch between the employee witnessing identifiable staffing and the employee witnessing case allocation. The formal bridge closes that gap only with explicit decision-specific identification. This is useful model-level clarification; significance and source fidelity still require external scholarly assessment.
