# Codebook 0.2

## Evidence status

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| documented | The reviewed material explicitly describes the scoped provision; it has not been operationally tested. |
| not_described | No description found within the recorded document set and search boundary. Operational existence remains unknown. |
| unclear | Language, scope, accessibility or conflicting materials prevent classification. |
| not_applicable | The question does not apply to the stated population or function; give a reason. |

Every code requires source IDs and locators, population/time scope and rationale. There is no default conversion from not_described to unavailable. A directly documented refusal or absence needs its own quoted/source-linked finding, not inference from silence. Known future or historical provisions do not become current facts automatically.

## Responsibility

An employer's name establishes institutional identity. A help desk establishes a contact. A recruiter role may establish a review function. An identifiable assignee requires an explicit connection between a particular case and its responsible actor, not a publicly listed employee name. Do not publish private candidate or staff information. The quick scan records institutional identity, functional role, case-assignee disclosure and candidate contact separately. These observations are not claims that Local Law 144 mandates public identification of individuals.

## Review and alternatives

A post-decision challenge needs a stated way to contest a screening decision and seek reconsideration. Generic inquiries, assessment assistance, retakes, a complaint about notice/audit compliance, data access or correction, and a general human-review statement are separately coded. They do not supply the missing route by implication.

Record the affected group: NYC candidates, all candidates, California residents, another stated group, or unclear. A geographically restricted privacy right is positive evidence for that group only. Record an accommodation request separately from an alternative selection process; alternative instructions do not prove the alternative must be granted. A statement of potential rights is a disclosed conditional right, not evidence that a request was accepted or a decision reversed.

## Six deep checks

1. Responsibility: assigned actor, case linkage and authority.
2. Rule: assessed criteria and audit information; distinguish disclosure from validity, fairness and a working process to change a problematic rule.
3. Challenge: access, submission instructions and review remit for an adverse outcome.
4. Delivery: actual case-linked transfer of the review finding, with receipt evidence.
5. Application: an authorized action changes the relevant data, rule or decision, with the scope stated.
6. Result: revised record and candidate notification; distinguish administrative correction from further employment consequences.

Capacity, documented procedure and observed execution are separate. This public-document study records the first two where supported; observed execution remains untested without case evidence. A successful review need not favor the applicant, and correcting a record does not guarantee employment.

## Legal and formal layers

- `legal_requirement`: normative requirement grounded in a cited provision and bounded by its scope.
- `agency_guidance`: official explanatory interpretation, not a new statutory premise by itself.
- `historical_audit_observation`: reported finding with dates, units and agency response.
- `employer_disclosure`: an employer/auditor statement, not independently established performance.
- `research_recommendation`: an additional proposed safeguard.
- `formal_consequence`: a theorem about declared definitions and assumptions.

N/B/H remain original interpretation policies. Do not map an unknown disclosure to a false Lean fact. Actual negative facts require evidence; hypothetical countermodels must be labeled as hypothetical.

## Frozen calibration 0.2 — 5 November 2026

The original definitions above remain. The five calibration reviews add these mandatory qualifications:

- Every evidence row records `tool_scope`, `stage` and `time_scope`. A provision for one assessment tool does not cover an employer's other tools automatically.
- `post_decision_review_nyc` is the legacy field name for a scoped opportunity to challenge a screening decision **or an assessment result**. The stage must say which. A documented result challenge does not imply a universal appeal of final rejection. Final reporting must separate these categories and primary-tool from any-tool coverage.
- Human review before a decision is a review role, not a post-decision challenge. Explicit further human review of recruitment decisions can establish a request route without establishing correction authority or completed correction.
- A conditional right to contest AI decisions is recorded positively in `other_scoped_rights`; use `unclear` for NYC availability when its geographic/legal conditions are unresolved.
- Profiling objections, requests for non-AI review and data correction remain positive scoped provisions. If timing or adverse-decision remit is unspecified, they do not automatically satisfy the outcome-challenge check.
- Historically published alternative instructions are not erased, but their historical date must remain visible; do not describe current provision as verified.
- `examine_rule: documented` records criteria/audit disclosure only. It does not mark the full substantive rule-investigation check satisfied.
- `apply_correction: unclear` can record explicit decision-change capacity or data-correction rights without an implementation procedure. It never means a correction was observed.

This freeze follows calibration, not preregistration. All five records have a dated same-assistant reconciliation entry. A later definition change requires an amendment and recoding of earlier records. Search results and source-byte preservation remain limited as stated in the protocol.
