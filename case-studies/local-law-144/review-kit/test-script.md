# Controlled workflow test — not yet performed

Use synthetic records in an authorized sandbox. Record the tool/version, institution-specific expected behavior, role permissions and timestamps before starting. Real candidate cases require appropriate authorization and handling; do not submit fictitious complaints to public services.

| Scenario | Setup | Expected observation | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positive correction | A supported input error; submitted challenge; assigned reviewer; authorized receiving team | Reassessment uses corrected input; disposition is recorded and communicated | Case ID, finding, receipt, authorization, before/after assessment, notification |
| Missing delivery | Same supported finding, but the authorized recipient does not receive it | Case stays open/stalled; no claim of completed correction; escalation is visible | Missing receipt and escalation record |
| Wrong authority | Finding reaches a person without the needed permission | Unauthorized change is blocked; case is routed to the empowered role | Permission check and rerouting record |
| Premature change | Application attempted before the required finding/authorization | Change is blocked or identified according to the declared procedure | Ordering/authorization evidence |
| Upheld decision | Reviewer determines no correction is warranted | Reasoned upheld decision and notice, without a fabricated favorable change | Reviewed evidence, finding and notice |
| Scope mismatch | Candidate invokes a right described only for another population | Staff identify applicable route instead of promising an inapplicable remedy | Scope check and accurate candidate communication |

Run positive and failure scenarios on the same baseline where meaningful. Record actual results without converting an expected result into an observed one. Retest after repair. Review access, delivery and authority failures individually rather than awarding a composite ethics score.

Only completed operational tests can establish workflow performance. Lean results will describe the separately encoded procedures; they do not verify the institution's logs or software by themselves.
