# Scope audit — checkpoint 0.12

This build implements the next agreed fixed target, C4. It does not expand the original V1 research contract.

## Concrete construction and criterion

Fairness.lean has two closed finite example constructors: a four-field numerical notice and a Governance instance. Bool indexes online and assisted-intake benefits applications, not just/unjust. Verified scores are both 60 and the cutoff is 50. The initial assisted-intake rule deducts 15; the paired repair removes that deduction. The calculation, including the objectionable feature, is truthfully disclosed.

The independently adopted case policy stipulates equal entitlement-relevant information and channel irrelevance. The displayed tests require channel-invariant awards and awards following the cutoff on the verified base score. No generic fairness predicate or decision-system abstraction is introduced. The 30 principal definitions remain the same as RESULT_COVERAGE.md: these two concrete constructors instantiate existing structures or provide fixed finite case data. Fairness criteria stay explicit formulas in theorem statements; this must not be used to hide future new domain abstractions.

## Evidence and interpretation

The initial case has an actual grant/denial contrast, affected/excluded high-impact applicant, identified case officer, and concrete appeal/correction channels. Both versions satisfy the existing narrow, broad and harm-remedy checklists. Those checklists do not contain the new fairness tests. The channel penalty violates both tests; removing it supplies a non-vacuous satisfying comparison. Assignment, identification, relevant score, cutoff, affectedness and channels remain fixed. Exclusion/harm facts depend on the changed award and need not remain fixed.

MH-016/019 (§104) and MH-038 (§109) motivate scrutiny of design choices and justice; they do not dictate these numbers or this fairness formula. I27/A34/A35 document that gap. Arithmetic transparency differs from successful normative justification: a stronger explanation requirement that already certifies the adopted policy excludes the initial case. No claim establishes full social justice, statistical fairness, actual institutional compliance or real-world discrimination.
