# Authority and significance audit

Date: 2026-11-01. This is a scope-control and evidence assessment, not independent peer review.

## Scope decision

The authority comparison adds no principal definition, policy or type alias. It reuses OversightSystem, the existing binding ConsultationProcess instance, the existing public-benefits decision and the previously satisfied person-facing fragments. Its sole new case constructor varies institutional recognition; technical control, remit, independence and review remain fixed. ## What has analytical value

The richer existing checks expose specific missing links: the identifiable staff member may differ from the actual case assignee; identical final control flags can conceal different physical outcomes; different appeal triggers accept different otherwise fixed cases; and recorded, amendable community input can influence an outcome while an override rule retains final control. These are traceable, reproducible examples of specification dependencies. The public report explains them without requiring Lean.

Other checks are elementary consequences or modeling-independence tests. Exclusion without appeal violating an explicit appeal rule is a baseline consequence. The authority proxy deliberately separates capability from recognition. They are useful checks of the stated specification, not evidence of novel moral discoveries. Do not promote either into a flagship result or count its helpers as additional findings.

## Limits on the significance claim

The work demonstrates a limited method and a set of transparent case analyses. It has not established scholarly priority, major research novelty, real-institution effectiveness or a uniquely faithful interpretation of the source. The literature review is preliminary; no ordinary-reader comprehension test or external peer review has been completed. These limits belong in the final paper and report.

The original aspiration of 10–15 substantive positive results and 5–8 fixed countermodels has not been met. Only C2, C3 and C6 of the six fixed countermodel questions currently have completed formal witnesses under their stated interpretations. Additional case separations do not replace C1/C4/C5. The publication claim must remain a limited interpretation-and-dependency study unless further evidence warrants a stronger claim; no theorem or definition padding is authorized.
