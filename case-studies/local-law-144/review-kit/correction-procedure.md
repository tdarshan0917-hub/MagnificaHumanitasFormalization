# Proposed correction procedure

Before use, enter the institution, tool/version, covered applicants, decision stage, applicable legal duties, role permissions, retention rules and proposed response deadlines. Deadlines in this procedure are institution-set unless a specific legal source establishes otherwise. This workflow itself is not a statement of LL144 duties.

1. Receive the challenge through the declared route, issue a case ID and acknowledge receipt. Offer the assistance applicable to the person's circumstances. Record the challenged assessment and source data version.
2. Assign an owner and identify the reviewer with remit over this case. Explain contact and escalation arrangements to the applicant.
3. Review the evidence. Record the reasoned finding: upheld, correction warranted, or more information needed. A review must not guarantee a favorable finding.
4. Send the supported finding to the role authorized for its remedy; record recipient acknowledgement. If a vendor must change data or recompute an assessment, identify the employer role that retains responsibility for completion.
5. Apply the authorized correction or reassessment. Record before/after values, reason, authorization and affected decision. Correcting an input, a scoring rule and an issued decision are different actions.
6. Verify the resulting record and inform the applicant of the disposition. Record any remaining hiring step separately; a corrected assessment is not a job offer.
7. Escalate missed receipt, inadequate authority, disputed evidence or overdue action using an assigned role and institution-set timeframe. Keep an audit trail; do not silently label a stalled case complete.
